# Credits

`interview-me` is adapted from [Addy Osmani](https://github.com/addyosmani)'s [`agent-skills`](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills) interview-me skill. The process is reproduced close to word for word: the hypothesis with a confidence number, one question at a time with a guess attached, the want-vs-should-want probe, the restate, the explicit-yes gate, the 95% confidence stop, the example, the rationalizations, and the red flags.

Changed for this repo: the frontmatter is rewritten for user invocation (`disable-model-invocation: true`); "When to Use", "Loading Constraints", and "Interaction with Other Skills" are removed; the terminal turn hands off to `planning` instead of listing downstream skills; "Output" no longer offers an intent doc; the cross-references point at this repo's chain instead of `idea-refine` / `spec-driven-development` / `doubt-driven-development` / `planning-and-task-breakdown` / `source-driven-development`; and the intent-doc red flag became a no-file-writes red flag.
